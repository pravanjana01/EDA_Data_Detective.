🧠 How EDA Works

Exploratory Data Analysis helps us understand, clean and visualize data before making meaningful conclusions.

📥

1. Collect

Collect the required dataset from a reliable source.

🧹

2. Clean

Check missing values, duplicates and incorrect data.

🔍

3. Explore

Study the structure and important patterns in the data.

📊

4. Analyze

Use statistics and correlations to understand relationships.

📈

5. Visualize

Represent important findings using charts and graphs.

💡

6. Conclude

Convert the analysis into useful insights and recommendations.
